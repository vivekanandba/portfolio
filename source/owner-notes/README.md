# Owner notes

First-person statements by the owner, recorded on the date in the filename, kept verbatim apart from
punctuation and the removal of filler words. They exist because some of the most formative experience
in this career — running a mechanical product end to end on a small company's shop floor — was never
written down by anyone else.

Rules (ADR-0013):

- Filename `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`; first line `Source: owner, YYYY-MM-DD`. Both are test-checked.
- The text is the owner's, not a paraphrase. Editorial clarifications go in square brackets.
- A site claim that rests **only** on a note renders with a visible _self-reported_ marker and cites
  the note's date in the content-file comment. Where a third-party record corroborates the same
  point, the page cites that record instead and the marker is dropped.
- The owner reviews a note before it is committed — these files are public.
