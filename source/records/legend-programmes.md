# Legend years — the records I still owe the site

The site dates and attributes the 2013–2018 material from what the two capability decks and the
public records say. Where they say nothing, a caption reads "date not recorded", a role reads
"supported" or "company programme", and a start month is an inference. This table lists every such
place. I fill the three blank columns from my own records and drop photographs into
`source/my-photos/<folder>/` (its README explains the manifest); each batch lands as a records PR
that updates the captions, the archive entries (`/archive/legend/`, PR-C) and the project pages.

- **Month / year** — `YYYY-MM`, or a year alone when that is all I have. "before 2013" is an answer.
- **My role** — one of `led` · `designed` · `delivered` · `supported` · `workplace` ·
  `company programme` (the closed vocabulary from ADR-0015; the site's `Role:` tags use it).
- **One sentence** — what I did, in my words. It becomes the caption.
- **Photos folder** — the id for `source/my-photos/`; `—` where photographs do not apply.

Rows 1–14 are the hub gallery (`/work/legend-technologies/`), 15–22 the other project pages and the
`/work/` order, 23–27 the slip-ring line, 28–38 deck items the site does not show yet (the archive
page catalogues them), 39–51 open questions. Test-checked: seven columns, unique folder ids
(`tests/source.test.ts`).
