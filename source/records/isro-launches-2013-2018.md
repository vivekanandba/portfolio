# ISRO launches while I was at Legend Technologies — Jan 2013 to Jan 2018

Every orbital launch and major test flight from Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota, during my
five years at Legend. I went to SHAR as a vendor guest more than once and watched vehicles lift off,
and this table is where I set down which.

Dates and payloads are from public records (Wikipedia's PSLV and GSLV launch lists, ISRO mission pages
and NASASpaceflight), checked on 2026-09-19. The right-hand columns are mine to fill; each answer that
lands becomes a caption, an archive entry or a line in a Writing post.

- **At the launch** — the ones I watched from SHAR as a vendor guest.
- **My involvement** — hardware or tooling I worked on that flew.
