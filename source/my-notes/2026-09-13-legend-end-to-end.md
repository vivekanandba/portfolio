# Owning a mechanical product end to end — Legend Technologies, 2013–2018

_Spoken to the assistant while planning the Legend-era pages; transcribed verbatim, with filler words
removed and punctuation added. Editorial clarifications in square brackets._

I had the privilege — even the opportunity — to do end to end of a particular mechanical product.

I used to go bid for projects. Since this is an MSME, a medium-scale company, I was actually
authorised, and I took that ownership, to do that. So I used to go bid for projects, do pre-sales,
price negotiations, then even go for technical bids. After that, go for all the technical discussions,
convince the customer and take up the projects.

Do designs on it, with me as another person alongside my team of designers — mechanical designers.
Once the product was designed, again there was a QA cycle for design. Along with that, I used to take
it for prototyping. After prototyping, production: we used to produce this in batches, as this was the
aerospace industry that I primarily worked under.

Once that is done, first-article proving used to be done, and each one of those productions had
multiple challenges that I would have personally listed. There were production-related tolerances and
GD&T tolerances that we needed to take care of in design. We used to do design for manufacturability,
design for testability — design for multiple such things [I could not recall the rest of the
"design-for-X" list at the time].

I used to go to the production floor: sit with the lathe operator, sit with all the different
operators — the CNC operators, the milling operators, the welding operators. I used to even go and
check on the quality team, whether the quality was up to the mark.

Once the drawings were finished, I used to even do process documents for each one of them, so that the
production and the quality team were able to take it up. Again, me in a personal IC [individual
contributor] capacity, and managing a team as well — so I was doing both.

Once the inspection and everything was done, we used to hand it over to the customer. First-article
proving used to be done for most of the tooling use cases, and once that was done we had the final
handover to the customer as well.

So I was involved end to end. That is the reason it gave me a lot of impetus on my own personal
entrepreneurial journey.
