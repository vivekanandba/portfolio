# The book my mentor inscribed, 23 September 2018

_Read aloud to the assistant from the book itself. Kept because it dates when we met, and because it
is the thought he gave me. He signed it with his first name; the name is withheld here, as in every
other note, until he is asked._

I just saw the book. It was mentioned there. Let me read out that quote:

> Dear Vivek, your decisions determine your future, not your current situations. Best of luck for a
> great future. Regards, [his first name], 23rd September 2018.

So most likely it was in 2018 that I met him, because it was a few months after that he sent me this
thought. [Earlier the same day he had said "I think I met him in 2017 only"; the inscription settles
it as 2018, some months before 23 September. The note of 3 October 2026 had "end of 2018".]
