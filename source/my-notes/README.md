# My notes

Statements in my own words, recorded on the date in the filename, kept verbatim apart from
punctuation and the removal of filler words. They exist because some of the most formative experience
in my career — running a mechanical product end to end on a small company's shop floor — was never
written down by anyone else.

Rules (ADR-0013):

- Filename `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`; first line `Source: my own account, YYYY-MM-DD`. Both are test-checked.
- The text is mine, not a paraphrase. Editorial clarifications go in square brackets.
- A site claim that rests **only** on a note renders with a visible _my account only_ marker and cites
  the note's date ("my note of YYYY-MM-DD"). Where a third-party record corroborates the same point,
  the page cites that record instead and the marker is dropped.
- I read a note before it is committed — these files are public.
