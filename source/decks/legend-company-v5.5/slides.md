## Slide 38 — HIGH PRECISION SLIPRINGS

## Slide 39 — TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER (TOT)

- Technology Transfer For Manufacturing Slipring By VSSC -Year 2000
- The technology obtained from ISRO had the following features of manufacturing Slip ring
- Contact between stator and rotor was using coin silver rings and silver Graphite brush blocks.
- Insulation between stator and rotor was done using modified epoxy.

## Slide 40 — UPGRADED TECHNOLOGY IN SLIP RING

- Slipring Assembly Rotational Test Rig
- Contact between stator and rotor is done using Beryllium copper wire and Electrode grade copper rings with U groove technology.
- Insulation between stator and rotor is done using special Epoxy potting compound with fixtures.
- Gold plating on Contacts [Rings & Brush wires].
- Printed Circuit Board [specially designed for application].
- IP [Ingress protection] sealing.

## Slide 41 — CHALLENGES FACED IN EARLIER TECHNOLOGY

- Silver Graphite powder formation after certain period of usage & it leads to short-circuit between the channels.
- Continuity problems faced between rotor & stator.
- Water ingress inside the slip ring.
- Frequent maintenance & Lubrication.
- Slippage of brush blocks between the channels.
- More power required because of friction

## Slide 42 — FEATURES OF PRESENT SLIPRING TECHNOLOGY

- Electrode grade copper and beryllium copper wire used as contacts (B/w rotor and stator) makes no powder formation & better conductivity..
- Gold plating to ensure high conductivity & long life.
- Use of PC board to ensure continuity & better assembly features.
- Ingress protection using sealants and O-rings to protect the slip ring from dust & water.

## Slide 43 — PERFORMANCES OF PRESENT SLIP RING

- Wear and tear of contacts is avoided.
- Continuity is ensured at all times.
- Potting compounds provides better insulation.
- IP rating makes the slip ring operate for longer time in all environment.
- Life of slip ring is increased to 15 years.
- Maintenance free.

## Slide 44 — DESIGN FACILITIES & CAPABILITIES

- The slip rings manufactured in Legend Technologies are designed in-house as per the requirement of customer.
- Use of Auto CAD and UG software for drafting and modeling operation..
- Availability of special skilled workforce to Design, manufacture, Test and overhaul precision slip ring.

## Slide 45 — SLIP RING MATERIALS & MACHINING

- Aerospace grade materials are used to manufacture the Slip rings.
- All the components are machined only in precision machining centers.
- Soldering process will be carried out by skilled workforce using High precision Soldering station [Micro].
